Written in the creator's own words wherever possible, each section dated. A
field nobody could answer says so; it is never filled in by guesswork.

## 1. Business and objective
What the channel sells (or that it sells nothing), to whom (B2C, small B2B,
large B2B), at what ticket, and the one objective the channel serves: leads,
authority, sponsor-grade reach, or product sales. The wrong viewer is a cost,
however many views they bring.

## 2. Audience profile
Gender, age range, geography and the market's own vocabulary, awareness level
(knows nothing / knows the problem / compares solutions), goals and pains.
Each line marked **measured** (Studio data, with source and date) or
**hypothesis** (declared, not yet measured).

## 3. Adjacent interests
Topics outside the core niche that the same viewer cares about. They widen
reach without changing who you speak to.

## 4. Content pillars
Four umbrellas, distinct from each other, each named by what the viewer walks
away with — never by a tool. Marked **provisional** until /competitors backs
each one with real outliers.

## 5. Differentiation
What this channel adds that the channels already winning with this audience do
not.
