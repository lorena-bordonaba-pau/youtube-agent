> **Fictional example.** An invented channel, "Espresso at Home". Not data.

**Stage:** market-validated

## 1. Business and objective — 2026-03-02
Sells a €49 online course on dialling in espresso at home (B2C). The channel's
one objective is **course sales**: a viewer who only wants gear reviews is a
cost, however many views they bring.

## 2. Audience profile — 2026-03-02
- Men 25-44, 78 % — **measured** (`yt_demographics.py`, 2026-02-28, `youtube_api`)
- US, UK and Germany lead — **measured** (`yt_geography.py`, 2026-02-28)
- Owns a machine under €600 and is frustrated by sour shots — **hypothesis**
  (declared by the creator, not measured)
- Awareness: knows the problem, is comparing solutions — **hypothesis**

## 3. Adjacent interests
Home barista gear, kitchen workflow, specialty coffee travel.

## 4. Content pillars — validated 2026-03-20 with `/competitors`
1. **Fix your shot** — diagnose sour, bitter, fast, slow. Backed by 3 outliers ≥3× on two competitor channels.
2. **Get more from cheap gear** — Backed by 2 outliers ≥4×.
3. **Milk you can pour** — Backed by 2 outliers ≥2.5×.
4. **Beans without the snobbery** — Backed by 1 outlier; weakest, review in April.

## 5. Differentiation
Every video ends with a shot that was fixed on camera, on a machine the viewer
can afford. The channels already winning with this audience review gear; none
fixes a real shot start to finish.
